Tidings

The birthdays, anniversaries, and little moments that matter — remembered, and answered with something warm.

How it works

1

Add your people

Add birthdays, anniversaries, and any occasion you want to remember — Tidings keeps the dates.

2

Get reminded in time

Tidings notifies you a couple of days before, and again on the day, with a message ready to send.

3

Send it, or automate it

Send it yourself in a tap, or turn on Auto‑Send with Tidings Pro to deliver it by text automatically, signed with your name.
